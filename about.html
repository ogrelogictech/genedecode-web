The man behind Deep Dives, and the mission of Truth, Trust in God, and Service.

Gene's story

Gene is a U.S. Navy submarine veteran, a martial arts Sensei, and a naturopathic practitioner. Through a series of life events he was drawn onto a path of seeking and sharing the truth and hidden knowledge of the world.

His mission is to help others in their personal and spiritual growth, and to build a community of like-minded people encouraged to know the truth that affects them on their journey into the Great Awakening. This work is aligned with the Blessed For Service ministry.
